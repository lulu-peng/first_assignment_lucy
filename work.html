Ideas made visible


A collection of design projects exploring branding, visual identity, editorial design, packaging, and digital experiences.

Each project begins with a question: What does this idea need to become?

From research and concept development to typography, colour, imagery, and final applications, I approach each project as an opportunity to create a visual language that feels intentional and meaningful.

I've done some surrealism booklet, rebranding and creating apps.

Featured Projects

01 — Shanghai Standard

Brand Identity / Art Direction

A visual identity for a Shanghai-inspired hair salon in Vancouver. The project explores the elegance and cultural character of 1930s Shanghai through a contemporary visual system, combining Art Deco influences, refined typography, and a modern approach.

Brand Identity · Art Direction · Print · Packaging

02 — Energy Drink Brand

Brand Strategy / Packaging

A new energy drink concept focused on a different idea of energy—one that connects performance with purity, simplicity, and everyday wellness. The project explores how a modern beverage brand can communicate energy without relying on the visual language of traditional energy drinks.

Brand Strategy · Naming · Visual Identity · Packaging

03 — Vancouver Travel App

UX/UI / Digital Design

A mobile experience designed to make exploring Vancouver easier for visitors. The project focuses on reducing the time and uncertainty involved in choosing restaurants, activities, and services while travelling.

User Research · UX/UI · Information Architecture · Prototypingh4

04 — McDonald's Annual Report

Editorial / Information Design

A visual exploration of McDonald's annual report, transforming corporate information into a more engaging and accessible editorial experience. The project combines structured information with a bold visual language inspired by the brand.

Editorial Design · Typography · Information Design · Art Direction

05 — Surrealism Booklet

Editorial / Typography

An editorial exploration of Surrealism and the artists who challenged conventional ideas of reality. The design uses typography, imagery, and composition to create a visual experience that reflects the unexpected nature of the movement.

Editorial Design · Typography · Layout · Art Direction

06 — The Five Love Languages

Book Jacket / Print Design

A contemporary book jacket design exploring how typography, composition, and visual symbolism can communicate the emotional themes of a familiar title while creating a distinctive shelf presence.

Book Design · Typography · Print Design · Art Direction

07 — Organic Thai Spa

Brand / Print Redesign

A visual redesign for a Vancouver Thai wellness business. The project explores how a refreshed visual identity and clearer communication can create a more contemporary and inviting experience while maintaining the warmth of traditional Thai wellness.

Brand Refresh · Print Design · Visual Communication

My Process

Research → Concept → Design → Refine

Every project is different, but my process usually begins with understanding the problem and the people behind it. I then explore ideas, develop a visual direction, and refine the details until the final design communicates clearly and feels cohesive.

More Than the Final Design

I'm interested in showing how a design idea develops , not just what it looks like at the end.

Sketches, research, experiments, typography, colour studies, and iterations are all part of the process—and each one helps shape the final result.
