About Me

Design is how I make sense of ideas

Hi, I'm Lucy, a graphic designer based in Vancouver, BC. I'm interested in how design can turn ideas, stories, and experiences into something people can see, understand, and remember.

My design work explores branding, visual identity, editorial design, packaging, and digital experiences. I enjoy working across different types of projects because every project brings a new story, audience, and problem to solve.

For me, design is more than making something look good. I believe strong design starts with curiosity and understanding.I like to research the audience, explore different ideas, and find a visual direction that feels both meaningful and purposeful. From typography and colour to layout and imagery, I pay attention to how every element works together to communicate an idea.


My Approach

01 — Discover

I begin by understanding the project, its audience, and the story behind it.

02 — Explore

I experiment with concepts, visual references, typography, colour, and composition to find the right direction.

03 — Create

I turn the strongest idea into a clear and cohesive visual system that works across different applications.

04 — Refine

I believe the details matter. I continue to edit, test, and refine until the design feels intentional and complete.


What I Love About Design

I'm especially drawn to projects where torytelling and visual design come together.Whether I'm developing a brand identity, designing a publication, creating packaging, or building a digital experience, I enjoy finding the visual language that makes an idea feel unique.

I'm inspired by culture, architecture, fashion, typography, travel, and everyday experiences. Living in Vancouver has also given me an appreciation for the city's mix of cultures, nature, and urban life—something I often bring into the way I see and approach design.

Let's Create Something

I'm always interested in learning, exploring new ideas, and collaborating on projects that have a story to tell.
